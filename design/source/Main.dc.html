HOW IT WORKS

From photographs to a structured report in four steps.

    Upload Product Images

    Up to five photographs — front, back, sides and close-ups of the label.

  2. STEP 02

    Extract Label Information

    AI vision and OCR read product, manufacturer, licence, dates and declarations.

  3. STEP 03

    Review Inspection Findings

    Check reference matches, packaging differences and labelling findings. Correct anything uncertain.

  4. STEP 04

    Generate PDF Report

    Record your decision and create a print-ready report with the original photographs.

ABOUT CRATE-SCAN

Built to support inspectors, not replace them.

Designed to support inspection workflows with structured information and evidence-based findings. Final inspection decisions remain with the authorized inspector.

  • CRATE-SCAN is an independent inspection-support tool. It is not an official FSSAI or government portal.
  • Licence and product checks compare against a demonstration reference database, not live government records.
  • A packaging difference or a missing reference record is an observation for review — never proof of counterfeiting.
  • Every finding shows its evidence and status, and the inspector records the final decision.
